# MOOD 3D Diner

Create a **single-page premium futuristic 3D food website called “MOOD”**, inspired by the provided reference image. Do NOT create a generic 2D restaurant website.

### Design

* Cinematic dark black/charcoal background
* Premium glassmorphism
* Warm orange/amber glow
* Realistic high-quality burger and food visuals
* 3D depth, reflections, shadows and cinematic lighting
* Apple-style premium minimal design
* Smooth scroll, parallax and subtle 3D animations

### Navbar

**MOOD** logo with:
**Home | Menu | Offers | About | Contact**

Use a floating transparent glass navbar.

### Hero

Create a fullscreen immersive 3D hero featuring a **realistic juicy chicken burger** with steam, particles and orange cinematic lighting.

Text:
**“Good Food. Good Mood!”**
Make **“Mood!”** orange.

Subtitle:
**“Fresh flavors. Bold cravings. Delivered your way.”**

Buttons:
**ORDER NOW** | **EXPLORE MENU**

Add subtle mouse movement and scroll-based burger animation.

### Menu Section

Show premium realistic 3D food cards for:

* Cheese Burger
* Spicy Chicken Burger
* Double Beef Burger
* Crispy Chicken
* Loaded Fries
* Drinks

Include image, name, short description, rating and price. Use glassmorphism with hover/tilt effects.

### Offers Section

Create a cinematic featured offer:
**“Spicy Burger Combo – 20% OFF”**
Show burger, fries and drink with floating 3D presentation.

### About Section

Dark cinematic restaurant/kitchen scene with warm lighting and subtle smoke.

Heading:
**“Made Fresh. Made With Passion.”**

Add a short premium description about MOOD.

### Contact Section

Simple premium glassmorphism contact section with:
**Location | Phone | Email | Opening Hours**

### Final CTA

**“Ready for Your Next Craving?”**
“One bite is all it takes.”

Button:
**ORDER YOUR BURGER**

### Technical

Use **React + Three.js/React Three Fiber + Framer Motion/GSAP + CSS3**.

Keep everything as **ONE LANDING PAGE**.
Only implement the **Home, Menu, Offers, About and Contact sections** with navbar anchor scrolling.

Focus heavily on **realistic 3D food, cinematic lighting, glassmorphism, smooth animations and premium visual quality**.

Avoid unnecessary pages, dashboards, login systems, cart systems, product-detail pages or complex backend functionality. Keep the implementation lightweight to minimize credit usage.
